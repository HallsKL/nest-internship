Our hoodies are designed with a modern, slightly oversized box-fit layout featuring dropped shoulders for effortless urban styling. We recommend ordering your true size for that signature relaxed streetwear silhouette, or sizing down if you prefer a traditional, slim-fitting classic cut.

All product shipments leave our local fulfillment hubs within 24 to 48 hours of secure payment clearance. Standard domestic shipping typically takes 3–5 business days to arrive directly at your doorstep, completely tracked from transit to drop-off.

To ensure your heavy combed cotton fabric blend maintains its premium mid-weight fleece density and vivid color over time, always wash your hoodie inside-out in cold water cycles with similar dark tones. Hang dry naturally or tumble dry low.
