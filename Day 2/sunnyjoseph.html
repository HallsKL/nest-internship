Sunny Joseph

Biography

Sunny Joseph is a senior Congress leader and MLA from the Peravoor constituency in Kannur district. He began his political career through student activism and gradually became an important figure within the Kerala Congress organization. He has served in several party positions and is known for maintaining a strong connection with grassroots workers and local communities.

Political Career

During the 2026 Kerala Assembly Election, Sunny Joseph successfully retained his seat and became one of the trusted leaders in the UDF government. After the election victory, he was included in the ministry and was given responsibility in important administrative departments. He is recognized for his calm leadership style, organizational skills, and focus on development projects, especially in northern Kerala. Supporters view him as a disciplined and loyal Congress leader who worked consistently for strengthening the party.
