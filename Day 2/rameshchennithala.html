Ramesh Chennithala

Biography

Ramesh Chennithala is one of the most experienced leaders in the Kerala Congress and Indian National Congress. Born in Alappuzha district, he entered politics through student organizations and later became a major youth leader in the Congress party. Over the years, he served as President of the Kerala Pradesh Congress Committee (KPCC), Home Minister of Kerala, and Leader of the Opposition in the Kerala Legislative Assembly.

Political Career

In the 2026 Kerala Assembly Election, he won again from the Haripad constituency and played an important role in the UDF’s return to power after ten years. Because of his long administrative experience and strong influence in Kerala politics, he was given a major role in the new cabinet led by V.D. Satheesan. Chennithala is especially known for speaking strongly on corruption issues, governance reforms, and law-and-order matters. He has remained one of the senior-most faces of the Congress party in Kerala for many years.
