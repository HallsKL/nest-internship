V.D. Satheesan

Leader of the Opposition, Kerala Legislative Assembly

Biography

V.D. Satheesan is a prominent leader representing the Paravur constituency in Ernakulam district. Known for his sharp legislative skills and articulate arguments, he has been serving as an elected Member of the Legislative Assembly (MLA) consistently.

Key Responsibilities

  • Leader of Opposition in the Kerala State Assembly.
  • Advocating for legislative policy updates and public welfare initiatives.
  • Representing constituent grievances and leading floor debates.
