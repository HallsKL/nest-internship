Shibu Baby John

Biography

Shibu Baby John is a senior leader of the Revolutionary Socialist Party (RSP), a socialist political party in Kerala that has often been allied with the UDF. He is the son of former RSP leader Baby John, who was an important political figure in Kerala’s labour movement.

Political Career

Shibu Baby John became popular through his active involvement in labour rights, workers’ welfare, and coastal community issues. He represented the Chavara constituency and served previously as Labour Minister in Kerala. In the 2026 Kerala Assembly Election, he returned as an important UDF leader and joined the new cabinet. He is known for maintaining close relationships with trade unions and ordinary workers. His political image is strongly connected with social justice and labour welfare policies.
