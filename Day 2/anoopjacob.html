Anoop Jacob

Biography

Anoop Jacob is the leader of Kerala Congress (Jacob), a regional political party that is part of the United Democratic Front (UDF). He is the son of the late T. M. Jacob, a respected Kerala politician and former minister. After his father’s death, Anoop Jacob entered active politics and became one of the important young alliance leaders in the UDF.

Political Career

He represents the Piravom constituency and is known for his focus on local development, infrastructure improvement, and public welfare projects. In the 2026 Kerala election, he retained strong support in his constituency and continued to represent Kerala Congress (Jacob) within the UDF coalition. Following the UDF victory, he was included in the ministry as a representative of his party. He is especially popular among Christian community voters in central Kerala and is viewed as a practical and development-oriented politician.
