K. Muraleedharan

Biography

K. Muraleedharan is a veteran Congress politician and the son of former Kerala Chief Minister K. Karunakaran, one of the most influential leaders in Kerala’s political history. Muraleedharan has had a long political journey, serving as MLA, MP, KPCC President, and minister in different governments.

Political Career

He is known for his strong public speaking skills and his deep influence among traditional Congress supporters. In the 2026 Assembly Election, he won from the Vattiyoorkavu constituency and became an important senior member of the UDF leadership team. His political experience and family legacy continue to give him a major role in Kerala politics. Muraleedharan is often seen as a leader who can strongly defend the Congress party during political debates and election campaigns.
